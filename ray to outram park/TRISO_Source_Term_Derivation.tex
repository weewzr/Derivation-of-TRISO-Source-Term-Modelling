\begin{abstract}
This work derives and verifies diffusion-based source-term models for a concentric five-layer TRISO particle. Method~1 develops the continuous spherical diffusion equations and analytical/modal structure. Method~2 develops an independently verified conservative finite-volume (FV) reference, the production walk-on-spheres (WOS) formulation, exact spherical first-passage kernels, an accelerated exact-interface renewal process, and a finite-state Markov-renewal transform reduction. Controlled two-layer and three-layer verification establishes the numerical building blocks. The frozen five-layer Cs-137 matrix has been constructed and diagnosed, but ordinary double precision is strongly affected by near-recurrent interface dynamics; a predeclared 50/80/120-decimal-digit high-precision cross-check passes the original numerical gates; this hard-matrix result is pending independent review. No final five-layer release CDF is claimed, and R2-WOS-02 and R2-B01 remain open.
\end{abstract}

\section{Geometry and scope}
The concentric regions satisfy
\begin{equation}
0=R_0<R_1<R_2<R_3<R_4<R_5=R,
\end{equation}
corresponding to kernel, buffer, IPyC, SiC and OPyC. For the frozen five-layer Cs-137 benchmark,
\begin{equation}
(R_1,\ldots,R_5)=(212.5,312.5,352.5,387.5,427.5)\;\si{\micro\metre},
\end{equation}
and
\begin{equation}
\bm D=(1.2502983\times10^{-13},10^{-8},4.0622991\times10^{-14},
9.2277732\times10^{-17},4.0622991\times10^{-14})\;\si{\metre\squared\per\second}.
\end{equation}
The project has three active methods: first-principles analytical diffusion; first-principles computational diffusion (FV and WOS/first-passage); and a later semi-empirical mechanistic/reduced-order method. Only Methods~1--2 are developed here.

\section{Governing problem}
For piecewise constant $D(r)$,
\begin{equation}
\frac{\partial c}{\partial t}
=\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}
\left(r^2D(r)\frac{\partial c}{\partial r}\right)+S-R(c).
\label{eq:pde}
\end{equation}
Centre regularity is
\begin{equation}
c_r(0,t)=0.
\end{equation}
For the ideal $K=1$ verification interfaces,
\begin{align}
c_i(R_i,t)&=c_{i+1}(R_i,t),\\
D_i c_{i,r}(R_i^-,t)&=D_{i+1}c_{i+1,r}(R_i^+,t).
\end{align}
The general continuum exterior may be Robin,
\begin{equation}
-D_5c_r(R,t)=h[c(R,t)-c_\infty],
\end{equation}
whereas the frozen production-WOS benchmark is absorbing:
\begin{equation}
c(R,t)=0.
\end{equation}
Its initial condition is
\begin{equation}
c(r,0)=
\begin{cases}
c_0,&0\le r<R_1,\\
0,&R_1<r<R,
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
with no continuing source after $t=0$.

\section{Method 1: analytical diffusion}
Within a homogeneous layer,
\begin{equation}
c_t=D\left(c_{rr}+\frac{2}{r}c_r\right).
\end{equation}
For the source-driven homogeneous benchmark write $c=w+v$. Separation $v=\phi(r)T(t)$ yields
\begin{equation}
\frac{1}{r^2}\frac{d}{dr}\left(r^2\phi'\right)+k^2\phi=0,
\qquad T=e^{-Dk^2t}.
\end{equation}
With $u=r\phi$,
\begin{equation}
u''+k^2u=0.
\end{equation}
For the multilayer problem a common decay rate $\Lambda$ gives
\begin{equation}
k_i^2=\frac{\Lambda}{D_i},
\end{equation}
and the interface/boundary equations assemble
\begin{equation}
\bm M(\Lambda)\bm a=0,\qquad \det\bm M(\Lambda)=0.
\end{equation}
The repository directly establishes the piecewise self-adjoint boundary/interface cancellation and weighted orthogonality. Full singular piecewise-transmission completeness and numerical modal truncation remain qualified.

\section{Method 2A: conservative deterministic FV reference}
For spherical control volume $i$,
\begin{equation}
V_i\frac{dc_i}{dt}
=G_{i-\frac12}(c_{i-1}-c_i)+G_{i+\frac12}(c_{i+1}-c_i),
\end{equation}
where
\begin{equation}
V_i=\frac{4\pi}{3}(r_{i+\frac12}^3-r_{i-\frac12}^3).
\end{equation}
The verified discontinuous-$D$ face conductance is
\begin{equation}
G_f=\frac{4\pi r_f^2}
{\dfrac{r_f-r_L}{D_L}+\dfrac{r_R-r_f}{D_R}}.
\end{equation}
Forward Euler is
\begin{equation}
c_i^{n+1}=c_i^n+\frac{\Delta t}{V_i}
\left[G_{i-\frac12}(c_{i-1}^n-c_i^n)+G_{i+\frac12}(c_{i+1}^n-c_i^n)\right].
\end{equation}
Independent audit established approximately second-order spatial convergence for the aligned benchmark, first-order temporal self-convergence for Forward Euler, and inventory closure to floating-point scale. These are benchmark-specific results.

\section{Method 2B: production WOS}
Uniform-volume kernel births obey
\begin{equation}
r=R_1U^{1/3}.
\end{equation}
For an interface-free WOS sphere of radius $\rho$,
\begin{equation}
\tau=\theta\frac{\rho^2}{D}.
\end{equation}
At capture distance $\epsilon$, the frozen $K=1$ interface law is
\begin{equation}
p_{i\to j}=\frac{D_j}{D_i+D_j},
\end{equation}
with zero interface-event time and reinsertion
\begin{equation}
\delta=\alpha\epsilon.
\end{equation}
Outer-surface arrival is release. Step-cap exhaustion is explicit censoring, never known non-release. Controlled WOS/FV verification supports a finite-$\epsilon$ statistical plateau; no accelerated-estimator epsilon convergence order is claimed.

\section{Exact spherical first-passage kernels}
For shell $a<r<b$, $T$ is first exit and $\lambda=\sqrt{s/D}$. The joint transforms are
\begin{align}
G^+(r,s)&=\mathbb E_r[e^{-sT}\mathbf1_{\{R_T=b\}}]
=\frac{b}{r}\frac{\sinh[\lambda(r-a)]}{\sinh[\lambda(b-a)]},\\
G^-(r,s)&=\frac{a}{r}\frac{\sinh[\lambda(b-r)]}{\sinh[\lambda(b-a)]}.
\end{align}
At $s\to0$,
\begin{equation}
P(R_T=b)=\frac{b(r-a)}{r(b-a)},\qquad
P(R_T=a)=\frac{a(b-r)}{r(b-a)}.
\end{equation}
For a centred ball,
\begin{equation}
H(r,s)=\frac{b}{r}
\frac{\sinh(r\sqrt{s/D})}{\sinh(b\sqrt{s/D})},
\end{equation}
with the regular $r\to0$ limit. Exit probabilities, conditional moments and conditional CDFs were verified against analytical references and direct supervisor-WOS sampling.

\section{Accelerated exact-interface renewal}
Process A is finite-capture production WOS. Process B replaces homogeneous-region wandering by the exact first-passage kernels while retaining the production interface law, zero event time and $\alpha\epsilon$ reinsertion.

A and B are not identical. For the controlled two-layer case,
\begin{equation}
1-(1-\epsilon/a)^3=0.005988008
\end{equation}
of uniform-volume births lie in the capture shell for $a=\SI{50}{\micro\metre}$ and $\epsilon=\SI{0.1}{\micro\metre}$. Their executed release-CDF discrepancy is compatible at the declared controlled precision, but only empirically. The corrected accelerated two-layer process agrees with FV at Monte-Carlo scale and reduces event count by approximately $8.68\times$ relative to direct 100-nm WOS.

\section{Five-layer computational pathology}
Direct production WOS censored all tested five-layer histories at the step cap. A bounded exact-shell accelerated diagnostic also produced 0/16 releases and 16/16 censored histories; almost all 1.6 million renewals occurred in Buffer, only two histories reached IPyC, and none reached SiC/OPyC. Thus rare interface-state recurrence, not homogeneous-region wandering, is the dominant remaining computational obstacle.

\section{Interface-state Markov-renewal reduction}
The four internal interfaces define eight post-interface states $S_0,\ldots,S_7$ plus absorbing release. Define
\begin{equation}
\Phi_i(s)=\mathbb E_i[e^{-sT_{\rm release}}].
\end{equation}
The first-step equations are
\begin{equation}
\bm\Phi(s)=\bm K(s)\bm\Phi(s)+\bm B(s),
\end{equation}
hence
\begin{equation}
\boxed{\bm\Phi(s)=[\bm I-\bm K(s)]^{-1}\bm B(s)}.
\label{eq:renewal}
\end{equation}
Process C is this deterministic matrix reduction of B:
\begin{equation}
B=C\quad\text{(mathematical reduction target)},
\end{equation}
while
\begin{equation}
A\approx B/C\quad\text{(controlled empirical compatibility only)}.
\end{equation}
For uniform kernel births,
\begin{equation}
\Phi_{\rm init}(s)=
\int_0^{R_1}\frac{3r^2}{R_1^3}H(r,s)
[p_{K\to K}\Phi_0+p_{K\to B}\Phi_1]\,dr.
\end{equation}

\section{Controlled matrix verification}
The controlled $2\times2$ matrix reproduced explicit B within approximately 1.004 Monte-Carlo standard errors at all predeclared transform points.

A genuine four-state benchmark,
\begin{equation}
R=(50,75,100)\;\si{\micro\metre},\qquad
D=(1,2,5)\times10^{-9}\;\si{\metre\squared\per\second},
\end{equation}
was then compared with $N=20{,}000$ explicit B histories and a refined FV reference. The maximum B/C transform discrepancy was 0.729 MC standard errors. The B/FV CDF RMS difference was $1.0395\times10^{-3}$ and the maximum absolute difference was $1.7794\times10^{-3}$. FV refinement and conservation errors were much smaller than stochastic uncertainty. Independent audit cleared the bounded hard-matrix diagnostic.

\section{Hard five-layer transform diagnostic: result pending independent review}
The hard $8\times8$ matrix was evaluated at
\begin{equation}
s=(0,10^{-9},10^{-8},10^{-7},10^{-6},10^{-5},10^{-4})\;\si{\per\second}.
\end{equation}
At $s=0$,
\begin{equation}
\rho[\bm K(0)]\approx0.999999999949649,\qquad
1-\rho\approx5.04\times10^{-11}.
\end{equation}
Ordinary f64 arithmetic gave
\begin{equation}
\Phi_{\rm init}(0)\approx0.999999887846684,
\end{equation}
missing the predeclared $10^{-9}$ normalization criterion. At $s=10^{-5}$ the residual was $1.138\times10^{-10}$, narrowly above the $10^{-10}$ criterion. The f64 Frobenius condition estimate is of order $10^{11}$ at $s=0$.

These failures were not waived. The predeclared 50/80/120-decimal-digit high-precision cross-check has now executed successfully. At the 80-digit reference, $\\Phi_{\\rm init}(0)=1$, relative residuals are of order $10^{-85}$, $\\rho[\\bm K(0)]=0.9999999999499492888$, and robust SVD gives $\\kappa_2(I-K)\\approx5.20\\times10^{10}$. The 50-to-80 and 80-to-120 digit results converge far beyond the original criteria. This is candidate evidence that the f64 failures were conditioning error; independent review remains mandatory before inverse-Laplace work.

\textbf{No inverse-Laplace recovery has been verified and no final five-layer release CDF is reported.}
